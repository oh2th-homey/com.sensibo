Sensibo makes your air conditioner, heat pump and air purifier smart. With this app, Homey controls your Sensibo Sky, Air, Air Pro and Pure, and uses the readings from Sensibo Elements and the Room Sensor, so the climate in your home becomes part of your everyday routines.

Warm up the house before you get home, switch the heat pump to fan mode when the fireplace is lit, start cooling when a room gets too warm, or turn everything off when the last person leaves. Temperature, humidity and air quality from your Sensibo devices can start any Flow in Homey.
